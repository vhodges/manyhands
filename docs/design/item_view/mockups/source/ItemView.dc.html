Wave 03 turns the Wave 01 and 02 foundations into something the team can use every day: a desktop app and a CLI that cover the full ticket and document lifecycle.

Goals

  • Dogfood Manyhands for its own planning from the first desktop release.
  • Keep every lifecycle action usable by keyboard on all three platforms.
  • Never lose an unsaved edit, even across a crash.

Track rules

Shared-library changes are reviewed and merged to main on their own. A Cycle is review-ready on its local Devenv gates; native CI runs after merge.
