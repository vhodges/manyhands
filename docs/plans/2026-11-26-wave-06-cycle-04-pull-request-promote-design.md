## Intent And Approval Boundary

In a pull-request repository, make `document promote` synchronize and publish the document's branch and answer with the pull request link, never committing to or pushing the primary; the only commits on the branch are the synchronization's own merges. Promoting again while the link is outstanding synchronizes again and answers `already_applied` with the same link. A document counts as promoted only when the content proof shows that a primary holds the branch's content, so a squash merge counts and an edit of a merged document does not count before its own pull request merges. The pull-request promote is a mode of the existing document-promotion lifecycle (decision 1). Approving this design approves the Cycle document's Required Contract and the rulings below. It does not authorize implementation, pushes other than the ticket branch, or merging.

## Conventions

- Names are cited as `file` and `function`. Line numbers are approximate, at main `8e5d9832`, which holds Cycle 03; `src/repository/promotion/`, `context_retirement/`, `read/`, `remote/sync.rs`, `remote/poll.rs`, `pull_request_links.rs`, `discovery.rs`, `cleanup.rs`, `forge.rs` and `canonical.rs` did not change after `bef571fd`, so their lines are as first read. Task 0 re-checks every one.
- "Pull-request repository" means a valid configuration with `integration = "pull_request"` (Wave 06 Cycle 01), which is `format_version = 2` and always names a publication remote.
- "The local primary" is `refs/heads/<primary>`. "The tracking primary" is `refs/remotes/<publication remote>/<primary>`, as last fetched.
- "The branch" is `manyhands/document/<ULID>`. "`L`" is its local tip. "`R`" is its remote tracking tip, `refs/remotes/<publication remote>/manyhands/document/<ULID>`, when that ref exists.
- "The proof" is `context_retirement/proof.rs` `preservation` (Baseline Fact 11). "The primary holds the document" is the rule R-3 of Part B.
- "A link row" is the document's row in `pull_request_links` (kind `document`). "An outstanding link" is a link row while the primary does not hold the document (decision 3).
- "An active context" of a document is the one its read comes from when the index's row for the document has `context.kind` `active`: its own worktree on the branch.
- "A branch record" is a lifecycle record whose path is `RecordPath::Branch` (Cycle 03). "A pull-request promotion" is a `document_promotions` row whose `integration_mode` is `pull_request`. "A direct promotion" is today's.
- "A child that completed" is a context child whose synchronization finished (`published` or `already_current`). "A child that ended" is one that `ends_context_child` names, or a branch gone after its child (`BranchEnd`). "A child that stopped" is any other stop.
- "The consented tip" is `L` as the confirm's re-observation read it, stored in the record's own immutable column when it begins (Part A).
- "A refused record" is an `abandoned` row with a `blocked`, `external_change` or `reconfirmation_required` refusal and no answer: written by `refuse_record` when a confirm's revalidation fails, or by the runner's `abandon` of an existing row.

## Part B: Prepare

**Dispatch (R-2).** `document promote --prepare` reads the configuration. In a pull-request repository the observation is the pull-request observation, whose digest's domain is `manyhands pull request promotion v1`; otherwise it is today's, unchanged. Promotion's observation type carries the mode and the pull-request facts to the runner and to `begin_record`, as the closure's `ClosureObservation` carries its path (Part G item 12). Confirm re-observes the same way. A same-request retry finds its record and continues it in the record's mode.

**The primary holds the document (R-3).** It holds it when the proof for the branch's tip passes against the tracking primary, or against the local primary when the local primary is the tracking primary or an ancestor of it; and `R` is absent, equal to the tip or an ancestor of it. A local primary ahead of or beside the tracking primary holds commits no one else has (in this mode Manyhands never commits there), so it is not used. `R` ahead of or beside the tip is another clone's work this clone has not merged, and the document is then not held; after a child that ended with `remote_branch_deleted`, `R` reads as absent, because the fetch does not prune it (Baseline Fact 22). The rule is one function, `primary_holds_document`, in `promotion/observe.rs` (so the proof's module does not change), taking the repository, the tip to judge, the tracking primary's OID (or none), the primary branch's name, the publication remote's name, the item, and whether to read `R` as absent. Every `ProofRefusal` reads as not held. Prepare, confirm and `item sync` (Part F) use it with the full proof; `document show` uses the cheaper twin of Part E (decision 9).

**Prepare reads local state only**, with no network, lease or write. In a pull-request repository it decides, in this order:

1. not a document: `not_a_document`, as today; no context: `context_missing`, as today;
2. the blockers below;
3. the primary holds the document (R-3 on `L`): `already_applied` at prepare, with no data and no record (R-4), as Cycle 03's withdraw prepare answers `NothingToWithdraw`, through a promotion twin of that `Observed` shape (Baseline Fact 14). There is nothing to confirm; cleanup is retirement's;
4. the branch's remote history is `Deleted` (a fetch saw it published, then gone): blocked with `remote_context_deleted`, as today, whose way out is `item republish`;
5. a link row exists: the situation `again`;
6. otherwise: the situation `first`.

**The effects (R-24).** `first` and `again` preview `context_synchronized`, `context_published` and `pull_request_link`, as the RFC lists them: the synchronization may merge the remote's primary in and publish even when `L` equals `R`.

**The blockers** kept from the direct promote: `context_nonconforming`, `context_dirty`, `context_conflicted`, `conflict_pending`, `history_unresolved`, `primary_missing` (no tracking primary; the local primary is optional in this mode), a merge, rebase or cherry-pick in progress at the root (`primary_conflicted`, as Cycle 03's branch close maps it, `closure/observe.rs` about 1037-1041), and an unfinished promotion record of the document (`recovery_required`, naming its request: the binding's hold, `bind/lifecycle.rs` about 396-435, the CLI contract's decision 33). Dropped, because nothing touches the primary (R-6): `primary_not_checked_out`, `primary_dirty`, `primary_ahead_of_remote`, `context_behind_remote`, `integration_conflict`, `document_invalid_after_integration`, and the binding's `identity_required` with the runner's `require_identity`. `history_unknown` does not block (decision 10, R-5): the context child's fetch settles the history. Another unfinished synchronization of the branch is not a prepare blocker: the child answers `busy` at confirm (R-18).

**The digest (R-7)** binds the domain, the item, the document's path, the branch, the primary branch, the remote, `L`, `R`'s relation to `L` (absent, equal or behind is one state, since a push of `L` moves `R` to it; `R` itself is bound when it is ahead of or beside `L`), the situation (`first` or `again`) and the `pull_request` object the preview shows. It does not bind the tracking or local primary, which move. So a poll that first creates `R` at this clone's own push changes nothing, while a save, another clone's publication fetched, a link given, or for `first` a change of forge setting or remote URL (for `again` the previewed link is the row's) between prepare and confirm is `external_change` at confirm. After a merge fetched between prepare and confirm, confirm's re-observation finds the primary holding the document; that observation digests as its own shape, as Cycle 03's `unoffered` does, which no ready digest equals, so the CLI's boundary answers `external_change`, as for Cycle 03's withdraw (Part G item 15, C19).

**The preview's details** are a new `PreviewDetailsDto` variant, `PullRequestPromotionPreviewDto`, tagged `"type": "pull_request_promotion_preview"`, holding the item, its path, the branch, the primary, the remote, `L`, `R`, the situation and the `pull_request` object: the link row's for `again`, the one that would be built now for `first`.

## Part D: The Data, The Preview And The Contract

**The data (R-8).** A new `MutationDataDto` variant, `PullRequestPromotionMutationDto`, tagged `"type": "pull_request_promotion"`, with `item_id`, `publication` (`published` or `current`, from the child's category; null when no child completed), `primary_holds` (boolean), `pull_request` (the object, or null) and `blockers` (empty, or `history_unresolved` on that end), as `PullRequestClosureMutationDto` carries them, so the CLI's retry guard reads them. It is the data of every finished answer of a pull-request promotion: `ok`, `already_applied`, and the ended answers `remote_branch_deleted`, `recovery_required` (`history_unresolved`) and Cycle 03's `ContextGone` answer. A child that stopped carries the synchronization's own data. Its schema is `schemas/v1/pull_request_promotion_mutation.schema.json`, a `DATA_ROOTS` entry.

**The preview.** `PullRequestPromotionPreviewDto` (Part B), schema `schemas/v1/pull_request_promotion_preview.schema.json`, a `DATA_ROOTS` entry as `lifecycle_preview.schema.json` is. Its blocked prepares answer with the existing `lifecycle_blocked` shape.

**A prepare's `already_applied`** carries no data (R-4): in a pull-request repository it means the primary holds the document. It comes from a promotion twin of Cycle 03's `Observed::NothingToWithdraw` (Baseline Fact 14), answered in `Confirmed::preview` and treated at confirm as a state the consent was not given for.

**`from_stored` (R-25).** The unguarded `DocumentPromote` lifecycle arm (Baseline Fact 16) is guarded on the `lifecycle` tag, and a new arm guarded on the `pull_request_promotion` tag reads the new data, as close's two arms are; `document promote` joins the synchronization-conflict arm and the host-data arm, as Cycle 03's verbs are in them, so a stored stopped promote keeps its data. `operation resume` gains an arm guarded on the `pull_request_promotion` tag, as it has one for `pull_request_closure`, so a replayed resume that continued a pull-request promotion keeps its data instead of answering `internal_error` (Baseline Fact 16); `dto.rs`'s `from_stored` tests gain its row.

**The way out (decision 11, R-22).** `way_out.rs`'s `lifecycle_way_out` routes the new data through a promotion twin of `branch_route`: `remote_branch_deleted` gives `Republish` of the item, then `PrepareAgain` (not the same request: the record is finished), as a withdraw's does; `recovery_required` with `history_unresolved` gives `item resolve-history`, as `item sync`'s does. `document promote` joins the `SynchronizationConflict` route to `child_conflict`, so a stopped promote's conflict has its way out. It is keyed on the data, so the direct promote's, the close's and `item sync`'s way outs do not change. As Cycle 03 did for its branch records, the contract's answers table gains no row: D-1 extends its sentence that a pull-request close's other recoverable answers have no row of their own (Baseline Fact 16) to the pull-request promote, so `tests/cli/recovery_tables.rs` needs no row and no golden for it.

**No new result code, blocker, verb or operation action.** A pull-request promotion is a `document_promotions` row, listed by `operation list` as a `promote` with its step.

**Goldens (R-23).** One per output contract: `mutation_v1` `document_promote_pull_request_ok` (the data, over SSH); `cli_v1` `document_promote_pull_request_preview` (JSON and text) and `document_promote_pull_request_blocked` (JSON and text); `mutation_v1` `document_promote_pull_request_primary_holds`, the prepare's `already_applied` with no data, written without a network as Cycle 03's `ticket_withdraw_closure_already_applied` is (`tests/mutation_confirmation/conformance.rs`). `item sync`'s link for a document uses Cycle 03's `pull_request_link` data and golden. Each schema and its registrations land with the first golden that carries its data: the preview schema with the `cli_v1` preview golden (Task 5), the mutation schema with `document_promote_pull_request_ok` (Task 8), since the `primary_holds` golden has no data.

**Human output.** The preview prints its effects and the link's `human_line()`. The answer prints `publication: <state>` when a child completed, then the link's `human_line()` on its own line, or the fixed sentence `the primary branch already holds this document` when `primary_holds` is true; a prepare's `already_applied` in a pull-request repository prints the same sentence. A unit test in `src/bin/manyhands-cli/lifecycle_tests.rs` pins the three lines.

**The desktop.** Compile-level arms for the new preview and data (R-14). "Approve and merge" keeps its words until Cycle 06.

## Rulings

Each: decision — why — cost if wrong.

- **R-1** The pull-request promote is a branch record of `document_promotions` with one child, and `branch_step` or `end_branch` finishes it — decision 1, and nothing of Manyhands' own is written on the branch, so there is no closure-writing hook and no publication child — `finish_branch` and the publication slot are dead code for a promotion.
- **R-2** A record's mode is fixed when it begins; prepare chooses the observation by the configuration, and the pull-request observation's digest has its own domain — Cycle 01 decision 11, Cycle 03 R-2 — a switch between prepare and confirm costs a re-prepare.
- **R-3** "The primary holds the document" is the proof for the tip against the tracking primary, or against the local primary when it is the tracking primary or behind it, with `R` absent, equal or behind (and absent after `remote_branch_deleted`) — the RFC's content proof; in this mode the local primary only ever fast-forwards to the tracking primary, so a local primary ahead holds work no one else has; `R` ahead or beside is work the clone has not merged — a document whose remote copy has another clone's later saves reads not held until a synchronization merges them.
- **R-4** When the primary holds the document, prepare answers `already_applied` with no data and no record, as Cycle 03's withdraw prepare does — there is nothing to publish, cleanup belongs to retirement, and in a pull-request repository that code at prepare has one meaning — a caller learns the reason from the code and `document show`, not from data.
- **R-5** Decision 10's mechanics: the pull-request observation reads `Unknown` history as no blocker, and the context child's fetch settles it (Baseline Fact 4) — a branch deleted since is found at confirm (`remote_branch_deleted`), not at prepare.
- **R-6** The pull-request prepare drops the root blockers except a merge, rebase or cherry-pick in progress at the root, drops the integration blockers, `identity_required` and `require_identity`, and keeps `primary_missing` for a missing tracking primary; the local primary is optional — nothing touches the primary, and the child's own stops (identity included, for a merge of the primary into the branch) answer as `item sync`'s — a root that is dirty or on another branch does not stop a promote.
- **R-7** The digest binds `L`, `R`'s relation to `L` (absent, equal or behind is one state), the situation and the previewed link — the consent is to publish what was previewed and to give the link it showed — a save, or for `first` a change of forge setting, between prepare and confirm costs a re-prepare.

- **R-10** `item sync` reads a document's row and never writes it — decision 4 (it never creates a link) — `given_at` is renewed only by promote.

## Alternatives Rejected

- Handing the promote to `item sync`'s binding with no lifecycle record: decision 1; a stopped promote would be listed, resumed and replayed as an `item sync`, the separate-runner shape Cycle 03 left after three review rounds.
- Answering `already_applied` at prepare with no synchronization while the link is outstanding: decision 2; the pull request would lag the person's saves.
- A link that ends only when its row is deleted: decision 3; `document show` would offer a merged pull request's link.
- The full proof in `document show`: decision 9; it reads every blob of two trees on every show.
- Keeping `history_unknown` as a prepare blocker: decision 10; the usual path, `item sync` and then promote, would need a poll first.
- Recording the consented tip and ending a retired record through `ContextGone` instead of a retirement fence: decision 13; a garbage-collected tip would answer `context_gone` rather than "already applied".
- Leaving a stopped promote to Cycle 03's `interrupted` rule: decision 14; a promotion records no base, so it would wait for good.
- A blocker `already_promoted`, or data on a prepare's `already_applied`: there is nothing to do, which is `already_applied`, and Cycle 03's withdraw prepare already gives that answer with no data.
- Rebuilding the link on `again`: decision 2's "the same link".
- Previewing `context_published` only when `L` differs from `R`: the RFC lists it, and the synchronization can publish a merge of the primary.
- A new SSH case: decision 6.
