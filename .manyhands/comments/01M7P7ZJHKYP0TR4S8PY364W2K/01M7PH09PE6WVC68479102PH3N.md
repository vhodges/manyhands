## Change

- `pass::encode` sorts every object's keys before writing (`Value::sort_all_objects`).
- `handoff::report_image` now goes through `pass::encode`. A background scan of `src/` found this second case: `decode_frozen` compares the frozen report byte for byte, and at startup it runs before the candidate checks. Fixing `encode` alone left the owner's registry unopenable by a desktop-feature build.
- The scan found no other stored or hashed JSON whose bytes depend on `preserve_order`. Other `Value` writes are only parsed back and compared as values.
- No migration: sorted is what builds without the feature have always written.
