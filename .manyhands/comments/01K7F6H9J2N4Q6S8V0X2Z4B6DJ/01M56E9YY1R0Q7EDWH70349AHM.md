Task 6A checkpoint: `close_ticket` runs end to end over the generic
lifecycle runner. The details are in the
[execution ledger](../../../docs/plans/2026-10-17-wave-02-cycle-10-confirmed-ticket-closure-execution.md).

- **Public interface.** `CloseTicketRequest`, `TicketClosureResult`,
  `TicketClosureOutcome`, `ClosureRecording` and `TicketClosureError` (13
  variants), with `close_ticket` and `cancel_ticket_closure`. Every
  repository error stays `Repository(_)`, so it never becomes an abandoning
  consent refusal.
- **Lifecycle.** `TicketClosureLifecycle` forwards to the closure record,
  observation and checkpoint modules. New records carry the observed
  `closure_kind`. The runner skips the context child for a contained
  context.
- **The remote context never receives the checkpoint.** A manual
  synchronization of the ticket context is `Busy` from `closing` until
  retirement. A synchronization already running holds the checkpoint off
  (`Busy`, nothing written).
- **Found by the exit tests.** A process exit inside the checkout seam
  leaves the context ref's lock behind. The resume now refuses with
  `RecoveryRequired` and keeps the record. The lock is never adopted, as
  Cycle 09 does for the tracking lock.
- **Gate.** fmt and Clippy are clean. `--lib closure` passes 157 (+31),
  `promotion` 199, `remote::reservation` 52 and `cleanup` 46.
  `remote_reservation` passes 13 and `document_promotion` its 74 SSH cases, unchanged.
- **To review.** With a remote, the design's conflict guidance ("synchronize
  the ticket context deliberately") now means a local merge only. A pushing
  synchronization is refused while the closure is open.
